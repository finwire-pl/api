# Changelog

Changes to the public finwire API (`https://public-api.finwire.pl`) visible to API consumers.
All endpoints are under `/v1`. Breaking changes are marked **Breaking**.

## 2026-11-27

### Added

- MCP server 1.50.0: new tools `calc_occasional_leave`, `calc_court_case_costs`, `calc_vat_refund`, `calc_accident_compensation`; `calc_net_income` with `under_26`; `get_legal_limits` with building permit rules.
- `GET /v1/occasional-leave` - dataset `occasional-leave`: occasional leave (urlop okolicznościowy) under § 15-16 of the regulation on justifying absence from work and granting employees time off: paid days off for the employee's wedding, the birth of a child, the wedding of a child and the death and funeral of a relative by degree of kinship (2 days or 1 day, 0 for persons not on the list), pay per day and for all days from `gross`, table of all events. Parameters `event`, `relation`, `gross`.
- MCP server: new tool `calc_occasional_leave`.
- `GET /v1/court-case-costs` - dataset `court-case-costs`: cost of a divorce with property division (`case=divorce`, `estate`, `division` none/disputed/agreed, `noFault`) or of a consumer bankruptcy (`case=consumer-bankruptcy`) for one party, without a lawyer and with a lawyer at the statutory minimum rates: court fees (Court Costs in Civil Cases Act art. 26, 38, 76a, 79), lawyer rates (§ 2, § 4, § 8 of the Minister of Justice regulations), stamp duty on the power of attorney, trustee fee range (Bankruptcy Law art. 491^9).
- MCP: new tool `calc_court_case_costs`.
- `GET /v1/business-poland/vat-refund` - Polish VAT refund deadline (VAT act art. 87) for the JPK_V7 filing date (`filed`): every refund type (`standard` 40 days, `vat-account` and `accelerated` 25 days, `retail` 15 days from the return deadline for online cash register sellers, `no-sales` 180 days, `no-sales-secured` 60 days) with the count start, nominal end, last day after the Saturday and public holiday shift (Tax Ordinance art. 12) and its reason; optional `variant` (fills `selected`), `deductionMonth` and `quarterly` for the input VAT deduction window (art. 86(10)-(13)): periods with return deadlines and the correction years.
- MCP: new tool `calc_vat_refund`.
- `GET /v1/business-poland/accident-compensation` - dataset `accident-compensation`: one-off compensation from ZUS for an accident at work or an occupational disease (Act on social insurance for accidents at work and occupational diseases, art. 11-15): `mode=injury` PLN per percent of health impairment, the increase after a worsening (`previousPercent`) and for total incapacity (`incapacity`); `mode=death` compensation for the family of a deceased insured (`spouse`, `children`, `others`, `paidToDeceased`) with the equal share; amounts in force on the decision `date` from the yearly ministerial notice in Monitor Polski (or the statutory formula from the GUS average wage before the notice), with the notice used and the history of periods.
- MCP: new tool `calc_accident_compensation`.
- `GET /v1/legal-limits`: block `buildingPermit` (dataset `legal-limits`) - building a house without a building permit under the Construction Law: footprint and storey limits of a small house on a notification with building from delivery (art. 29 ust. 1 pkt 1a), objection period for a notification with design, permit decision deadline, recreation cabin limits (art. 29 ust. 1 pkt 16), completion objection period, and setback distances from the plot boundary usable in the transition period of art. 102a (`setback`, null after `transitionUntil`).
- MCP: `get_legal_limits` summarises the rules for building a house without a building permit.
- finwire Health Contribution Forecast (`finwire_health_contribution_forecast`) in the finwire index register: `GET /v1/finwire-index/finwire_health_contribution_forecast` - the minimum monthly voluntary NFZ health insurance contribution for the quarter whose base GUS has not announced yet, with range and accuracy of the method; history of ex ante forecasts per quarter. `GET /v1/legal-limits`: `voluntaryHealth.current`, `next` and `lastAnnounced` have `indexId` (set on forecast quarters). MCP server 1.49.0: `get_legal_limits` names the index in the forecast line.

### Added

Dataset `municipality-flat-prices` now links to https://finwire.pl/najtansze-mieszkania-w-polsce#gminy.
- MCP server 1.47.0: `get_municipality_flat_prices` with `around` (a voivodeship city) returns the suburban discount.
- `GET /v1/housing/municipalities` and `GET /v1/housing/municipalities/{teryt}` - dataset `municipality-flat-prices`: flat transaction prices per m² from notarial deeds (RCN) in every municipality (gmina) with deeds, as a local government unit (Warsaw districts and city delegations merged into the city): last 4 full quarters of the county (earlier when the county enters deeds late, `windowShifted`), deeds, median and quartiles from 30 deeds (`smallSample` = count only), median flat area and price, primary and secondary market, year-over-year change, deeds per 1000 residents; one municipality with quarterly history since 2010. Filters `voivodeship` and `q` (name); pages with `limit` (1-500, default 100) and `offset`, sorting with `sort` (`teryt`, `name`, `deeds`, `median`) and `order`; `total` and `next` (URL of the next page).
- MCP server 1.45.0: new tool `get_municipality_flat_prices`.
- `GET /v1/foreign-income-pit` - dataset `foreign-income-pit`: Polish income tax of a Polish resident on a salary from an employment contract in Germany, Netherlands, United Kingdom, Norway, Ireland, Belgium, Denmark, Austria, Spain, France, Czechia, Sweden, Italy or the United States: treaty method for the tax year after the MLI (exemption with progression or proportional credit), each monthly payment converted at the NBP table A rate of the last business day before the payment (PIT act art. 11a), 30% of the foreign per diem exempt for each day abroad (art. 21 sec. 1 pt 20), employee costs, credit for the foreign tax, abolition relief with its cap (art. 27g), tax due, surcharge or refund, return forms and filing deadline.
- MCP server 1.44.0: new tool `calc_foreign_income_pit`.
- `GET /v1/business-poland/start` - starting a sole proprietorship (JDG) and its first 3 years: steps with dates (CEIDG application, NIP and REGON, ZUS registration within 7 days under art. 36(4) of the social insurance act, VAT registration, business account, cash register), ZUS stages (start relief 6 months, Entrepreneurs' Law art. 18; preferential base 24 months, art. 18a; small ZUS plus, art. 18c; full ZUS) with monthly social and health contributions from the bases of each year, VAT exemption and cash register limits pro rata in the start year (VAT act art. 113(9)).
- MCP server 1.43.0: new tool `plan_business_start`.
- `GET /v1/business-poland/suspension` - suspending a sole proprietorship (zawieszenie działalności, Entrepreneurs' Law art. 22-25): ZUS contributions for the partial month (base divided by the days of the month, art. 18(9) of the social insurance act), minimum health contribution, earliest end of suspension, latest end for childcare suspension (art. 36aa), resumption month, ZUS/PIT/VAT deadlines moved to the next business day, VAT deregistration after 6 months.
- `GET /v1/business-poland/seniority-bonus` - seniority bonus (dodatek stażowy) by employee group (civil service, local government, teachers, budget sector): percent per year, cap, first year, amount from the base salary and the date of the next increase, with the legal basis per group.
- `GET /v1/business-poland/pay-transparency` - pay transparency obligations (Labour Code art. 18^3ca-18^3d, in force since 24 December 2025) and the transposition status of Directive (EU) 2023/970 with obligations by headcount.
- `GET /v1/labour-office-support` - labour office support under the labour market act (Dz.U. 2025 poz. 620): start-up grant, training and education loans as multiples of the GUS average wage, PFRON grant, and the sickness benefit after the end of employment (optional `employmentEnd`, `sicknessStart`, `sicknessEnd`: entitlement and end date under art. 7 of the sickness benefits act).
- `GET /v1/cash-loan-prepayment` - dataset `cash-loan-prepayment`: early repayment of a consumer cash loan (consumer credit act art. 48-50): interest saved with a shorter term or a lower instalment, maximum fee (1% or 0.5% of the repaid amount, fixed rate only, capped at the interest due, applicable only above 3 times the average wage in 12 months).
- `GET /v1/energy/appliance-costs` - household electricity use: kWh and monthly and yearly cost of appliances at the G11 marginal price from URE tariffs (average or selected operator).
- `GET /v1/series/fx-cross?base=EUR&quote=USD&range=1Y` - dataset `fx-cross`: cross rates computed from NBP table A mid rates, with history.
- MCP server 1.42.0: new tools `calc_business_suspension`, `calc_seniority_bonus`, `get_pay_transparency`, `get_labour_office_support`, `calc_cash_loan_prepayment`, `calc_electricity_usage`.
